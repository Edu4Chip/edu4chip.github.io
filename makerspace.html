Edu4Chip MakerSpace

About the MakerSpace

The Edu4Chip MakerSpace is a Discord community for everyone interested in chip design. Whether you are studying, working in the field, or exploring chip design as a hobby, you are welcome to join.

The community is open to BSc, MSc, and PhD students, researchers, professionals, and hobbyists. Connect with people across universities, industry, and the wider chip-design community to learn from one another and exchange experience.

Use the MakerSpace to:

  • Ask technical questions and discuss chip design.
  • Share projects, ideas, tools, and resources.
  • Find collaborators and connect with other enthusiasts.
  • Keep up with events and opportunities.

If you still cannot join, contact Luca Pezzarossa at lpez@dtu.dk for assistance or a replacement invitation.
